## 🎯 **Market Research Summary**

### **Market Size & Opportunity**

- **Global Market Size**: $2.7B in 2025, projected to reach $7.8B by 2032 (14.2% CAGR)
- **Target Market**: Small to mid-size law firms (5-50 attorneys) - strong SaaS appetite among firms <100 lawyers
- **Technology Budget**: 85% of small firms spend $10K or less annually on software
- **Revenue Leakage**: Average billable hours captured per lawyer/day is only **2.9 hours** (target is 5-6 hours)
- **Growth Drivers**: 62% of firms report increased client satisfaction with case management systems

### **Key Pain Points Identified**

1. **Time Tracking & Billing** - #1 workflow pain: Late/inaccurate time & billing entries
2. **Revenue Leakage** - Only capturing 48% of potential billable hours (2.9h vs 6h target)
3. **Implementation Blockers** - Data quality, user adoption, system integration challenges
4. **Client Growth & Retention** - 33% cite this as primary challenge
5. **Document Management** - Scattered files, inefficient retrieval, version chaos
6. **Economic Pressures** - 44% under pressure to reduce fees
7. **Technology Resistance** - 50% of small firms hesitant about disruption

### **User Preferences**

- **50%** prioritize "ease of use" in software selection
- **20%** want automation and workflow features
- **60%** use 1-3 software vendors (prefer consolidation)
- **Referrals** still #1 source of new clients (31%)
